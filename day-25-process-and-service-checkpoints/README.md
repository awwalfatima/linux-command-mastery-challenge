#What I practiced
I brought together the process, service, resource, and scheduling commands. I checked system uptime and memory usage, reviewed the status of key services, and checked for scheduled cron jobs. I also practiced using process and service checks such as ps aux | grep, systemctl status, kill -0, and journalctl to understand how I would quickly assess the health of a server.

#What surprised me
What surprised me was how many different pieces of information can be brought together to get a quick picture of a system's health. Instead of checking processes, services, resources, and scheduled tasks separately, I could combine the information into one operational snapshot that would make troubleshooting much faster.
